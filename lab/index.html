A pattern that draws itself

Eight-point stars in the tradition of Islamic geometric art, built with Hankin's method: from the middle of every side of a grid of octagons and squares, two lines leave at the same angle and run until they meet.

The grid sketches itself first, then the stars draw in thin lines from the center out, and a slow wave of light keeps passing over them.

It is plain SVG and CSS, written when the site is built: no script draws it, and with reduced motion it shows as a finished drawing.
